# Policies and company documents

You receive these documents by email. Put them in this folder:

- `fictional_company_profile.docx`: facts about the Lindenwerk group (size, locations, business,
  governance). Use them to decide what applies. In the policy documents, [Company] means the
  Lindenwerk group and [CORP] the German parent, Lindenwerk Technologies AG.
- `lindenwerk_regulatory_change_policy.pdf` (GR-006): the company's regulatory change and policy
  management process. This is the manual process your product should support.
- The compliance policy library (PDF): Business Conduct Guidelines (REG-001), Global Rules
  GR-001 to GR-004, Supporting Documents SD-001 to SD-006, the Compliance Handbook, local thresholds
  for gifts, entertainment and business meals, and the gifts and entertainment FAQs.

All documents are fictional or anonymized. Keep your repository private.
