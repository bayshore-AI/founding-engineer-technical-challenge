# Public Contracts Integrity Act

## § 1 Scope

This Act applies to undertakings established in Country B that take part in public procurement
procedures or negotiate contracts with contracting authorities, state-owned undertakings or public
universities in Country B.

## § 2 Benefits during procurement and contract procedures

(1) From the publication of a contract notice, or the start of contract negotiations, until six
months after the award or contract decision, an undertaking taking part in the procedure shall not
offer, promise or grant any benefit, whatever its value, to a person who prepares, evaluates or
decides on the award or contract.

(2) Paragraph 1 also covers business meals, hospitality, invitations to events, product
demonstrations and factory visits that include hospitality, and travel.

(3) Paragraph 1 applies equally to external experts commissioned by the contracting authority to
evaluate tenders or proposals.

## § 3 Register of benefits

Undertakings shall keep a register of every benefit granted to public officials in Country B,
stating recipient, authority, date, value and business purpose. Entries shall be kept for seven
years.

## § 4 Entry into force

This Act enters into force on 1 March 2027.
