# Ordinance on the integrity screening of sales intermediaries

## § 1 Scope

This Ordinance applies to undertakings that use sales agents, sales representatives or
distributors to obtain contracts from contracting authorities in Country B.

## § 2 Screening before engagement

Before engaging a sales intermediary, the undertaking shall screen the intermediary's integrity,
including its ownership, any links to public officials and any previous corruption-related
proceedings.

## § 3 Periodic re-screening

The screening under § 2 shall be repeated at least every twelve months for as long as the
intermediary is active in procurement procedures in Country B.

## § 4 Entry into force and transition

This Ordinance enters into force on 1 April 2027. Undertakings shall apply it to existing
intermediaries from 1 October 2027.
