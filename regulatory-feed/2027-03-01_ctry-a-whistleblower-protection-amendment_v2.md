# Correction to the Act amending the Whistleblower Protection Act

The Act amending the Whistleblower Protection Act published on 18 January 2027 is corrected as
follows:

1. In § 1, the words "within two months" are replaced by "within three months".
2. In § 5, the date "1 March 2027" is replaced by "1 June 2027".

All other provisions remain unchanged.
