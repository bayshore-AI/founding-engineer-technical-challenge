# Act amending the Competition Act

## § 1 Documentation of meetings with competitors

(1) Undertakings shall ensure that written minutes are prepared of every meeting with competitors,
including meetings within trade associations, stating the participants, date, place, purpose and
the topics discussed.

(2) The minutes shall be completed within five working days after the meeting.

## § 2 Retention

The minutes referred to in § 1 shall be retained for at least twelve years after the meeting and
shall be made available to the competition authority on request.

## § 3 Entry into force

This Act enters into force on 1 July 2027.
