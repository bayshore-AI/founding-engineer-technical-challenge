# Gifts and Hospitality Transparency Act

## § 1 Scope

This Act applies to undertakings established in Country C and to branches of foreign undertakings
registered in Country C.

## § 2 Disclosure of hospitality to public officials

Undertakings shall disclose, every six months and in a public register kept by the Country C
Integrity Office, all hospitality and gifts with a value above 20 EUR given to public officials of
Country C.

## § 3 Entry into force

This Act enters into force on 1 April 2027.
