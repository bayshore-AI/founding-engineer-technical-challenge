# Act amending the Criminal Code

## § 1 Benefits to public officials

Granting a benefit to a public official in connection with the performance of their duties is not
punishable if the benefit is socially adequate and of minor value.

## § 2 Entry into force

This Act enters into force on a date to be set by ordinance of the Federal Ministry of Justice.
