# Guidelines on gifts and hospitality for staff of credit institutions

## 1 Addressees

These guidelines are addressed to credit institutions and investment firms supervised under the EU
prudential framework.

## 2 Gifts register

Institutions should keep a register of all gifts and hospitality received by staff in control
functions, with a value above 50 EUR.

## 3 Application

National supervisors are expected to apply these guidelines from 1 September 2027.
