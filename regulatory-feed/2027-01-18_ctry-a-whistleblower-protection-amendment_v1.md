# Act amending the Whistleblower Protection Act

## § 1 Feedback to the whistleblower

The internal reporting office shall give the whistleblower feedback on the follow-up measures
taken within two months of confirming receipt of the report.

## § 2 Confirmation of receipt

The internal reporting office shall confirm receipt of a report to the whistleblower within seven
days at the latest.

## § 3 Anonymous reports

Employers with 50 or more employees shall design their internal reporting channels so that they
can receive anonymous reports and allow anonymous follow-up communication with the whistleblower.

## § 4 Annual statistics

Employers with more than 1,000 employees shall publish annual statistics on the number and type
of reports received through their internal reporting channels.

## § 5 Entry into force

This Act enters into force on 1 March 2027.
