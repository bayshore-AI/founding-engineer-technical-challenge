# Member Bulletin 01/2027

**Country B tightens hospitality rules for public contracts**

Country B has introduced new rules on benefits to public buyers. From 1 March 2027, members may no
longer give gifts to procurement staff while a tender or contract negotiation is open. Business
meals below the usual local thresholds remain possible.

Members will also have to keep a register of gifts to public officials for three years.

We recommend that members review their gifts and hospitality policies now. Further details will
follow in our next bulletin.

_This bulletin is a summary for members and is not legal advice. Please refer to the official
publication._
