# Act amending the Unfair Competition Act

## § 1 Vouchers and gift cards

(1) It is prohibited to offer or grant vouchers, gift cards or other cash equivalents with a value
of more than 30 EUR per person and year to employees of a business partner.

(2) Paragraph 1 applies irrespective of the purpose of the voucher, including vouchers for
training courses, events or food and drinks.

## § 2 Entry into force

This Act enters into force on 1 May 2027.
