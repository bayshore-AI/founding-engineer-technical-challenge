# Circular on the term "public official" in anti-corruption law

## 1 Purpose

This circular explains how the ministry interprets the term "public official" in the criminal law
provisions on bribery. It does not change the law.

## 2 Employees of publicly controlled undertakings

Employees of undertakings that are controlled by the state or by municipalities and that perform
public tasks, in particular municipal water and energy utilities and public transport companies,
are to be treated as public officials, regardless of the legal form of the undertaking.

## 3 Persons entrusted with public tasks

Persons who are not civil servants but are formally entrusted with public administrative tasks,
including interns and temporary staff, are also to be treated as public officials.
